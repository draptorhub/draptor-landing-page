Refund Policy

Welcome to Draptor Technologies! Please review our refund policy carefully to understand the conditions under which refunds may be processed for event registrations, workshops, and other services offered by Draptor Technologies.

Last Updated: 01 May 2026

1. Event Registrations

All registrations made for workshops, events, or training sessions are generally non-refundable unless otherwise specified.

2. Eligible Refund Cases

Refunds may be considered only if:

  • The event is cancelled by Draptor Technologies
  • Duplicate payment is made accidentally
  • Technical payment failure occurs with successful amount deduction

3. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds will be processed within 7–10 business days to the original payment method.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be provided for:

  • Failure to attend the event
  • Late cancellations by participants
  • Incorrect information submitted during registration

5. Contact for Refunds

For refund-related requests, users may contact our support team using the official contact information provided on the website.
