Privacy Policy

Welcome to Draptor Technologies! Draptor Technologies values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

Last Updated: 01 May 2026

1. Information We Collect

We only collect limited information required for event registration, including:

  • Name
  • Mobile Number
  • Email Address
  • Selected Event/Workshop Category

2. Purpose of Data Collection

The collected information is used for:

  • Event registration
  • Participant communication
  • Event updates and notifications
  • Customer support

3. Data Protection

We take reasonable security measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information with third parties. Information may only be shared:

  • With payment gateway providers for transaction processing
  • If required by law or legal authorities

5. Payment Information

Draptor Technologies does not store users’ card, banking, or payment credentials. Payments are securely processed through authorized third-party payment gateways.

6. Communication

By registering on our website, users agree to receive:

  • Event-related updates
  • Registration confirmations
  • Important notifications

7. Changes to Policy

Draptor Technologies may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Updated versions will be posted on the website.

8. Contact

For privacy-related concerns, users may contact us through the official contact details available on our website.
